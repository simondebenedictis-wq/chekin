Company-wide metrics (ProfitWell)
"Lifetime Expansion (estimated)" and "Expected ARR at Renewal (estimated)" are calculated from Stripe invoice history, not official Stripe/ProfitWell metrics. Monthly MRR columns come from paid Stripe invoices grouped by month ("from invoice history") — a blank cell means no paid invoice that month, not zero. "Contact Owner" and "Company Owner" are looked up separately in HubSpot and are often different people — a contact's owner is not assumed to be the same as its associated company's owner. Columns marked "N/A — no data source" (next scheduled expansion/downgrade dates, forecasted MRR, renewal probability) have no real data source via the connected Stripe/ProfitWell/HubSpot APIs and are never estimated or fabricated.
